Sort Mechanisms

The single operation each algorithm repeats — traced through one concrete pass, with real numbers, not placeholder boxes.

1

Insertion Sort

The inner loop, once: pull the next key out, slide everything larger than it one slot right, drop the key into the gap.

2

Quicksort

One partition pass: two pointers walk inward, swap whatever is on the wrong side of the pivot, and stop when they cross.

3

Heapsort

Remove the root, promote the last leaf into its place, then sink that leaf back down until the heap property holds again.
